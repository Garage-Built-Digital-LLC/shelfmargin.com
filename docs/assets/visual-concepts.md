# ShelfMargin UI Visual Concepts

Purpose: explore 10 possible visual directions before generating a new logo, color system, app imagery, icons, and marketing assets.

## 1. Field Ops Ledger

Practical, fast, and work-focused. The UI feels like a sourcing clipboard, receipt printer, and scanner console merged into one clean app.

- Palette: off-white, graphite, receipt gray, green profit, amber caution
- Texture: subtle paper grain, ruled-list dividers, thin receipt borders
- Best for: scanner dashboard, buy list, CSV export, profit decisions
- Asset direction: product screenshots on real shelves, close-up scanner beam, receipt-style empty states
- Risk: can feel too plain if the hero and logo are not strong

## 2. Modern Book Scout

Warmer and more consumer-friendly. The app feels like a polished tool for solo sellers working in thrift stores, library sales, and used-book shops.

- Palette: warm paper, deep forest, muted blue, soft gold, clean white
- Texture: soft shadows, book cloth details, subtle shelf photography
- Best for: public site, onboarding, app store screenshots
- Asset direction: real sourcing environments, phone in hand, small stacks of books, clear readable app screen
- Risk: can drift into lifestyle photography unless the scan/profit UI stays dominant

## 3. Barcode Command Center

Sharp, high-contrast, and scanner-first. The UI makes the barcode and decision state the main visual language.

- Palette: near-black, white, electric blue, signal green, warning yellow
- Texture: scan lines, barcode rhythm, dark panels, strong numeric readouts
- Best for: mobile app scanner screen, dashboard, power-user workflow
- Asset direction: abstract barcode-based hero, dark scanner panels, animated scan beam, bold decision cards
- Risk: may feel too technical or harsh for new sellers

## 4. Used Book Marketplace

Trustworthy resale math with a marketplace-analysis feel. This makes the app look like a pricing intelligence product, not just a scanner.

- Palette: ivory, ink navy, emerald, muted red, brass accent
- Texture: clean tables, price tags, market cards, restrained chart visuals
- Best for: pricing page, Amazon-data positioning, profit analytics
- Asset direction: UI screenshots showing rank/profit/confidence, book price tags, simple market comparison graphics
- Risk: could look too spreadsheet-heavy if not balanced with real sourcing imagery

## 5. Garage Built Utility

Lean, founder-built, no-nonsense. The design feels like a dependable small-business tool instead of a venture-backed SaaS template.

- Palette: bone, charcoal, shop blue, utility orange, steel gray
- Texture: labels, stamped marks, simple line icons, utilitarian cards
- Best for: brand personality, beta product, credibility with practical users
- Asset direction: label-style logo, scanner/toolbench imagery, simple field checklist illustrations
- Risk: needs restraint so it does not become too industrial for books

## 6. Library Sale Pro

Quiet, organized, and book-native. The UI borrows from library systems, catalog cards, shelf tags, and checkout slips.

- Palette: catalog cream, library green, dark brown-black, faded red, label yellow
- Texture: catalog cards, index tabs, shelf-label strips, light paper noise
- Best for: browse/check-books flows, saved lists, empty states
- Asset direction: illustrated catalog cards, shelf tags, organized book rows, phone scanning a back-cover ISBN
- Risk: can feel nostalgic unless the app UI remains crisp and modern

## 7. Profit Signal

Focused on instant decisions. Every screen makes buy/pass/check feel obvious through big color states and simple motion cues.

- Palette: clean white, dark slate, vivid green, caution amber, stop red, small blue support accent
- Texture: minimal, card-free sections, large verdict bands, scan animation
- Best for: mobile webapp, iOS app, repeated field use
- Asset direction: big decision UI mockups, scan-to-verdict sequence, visual proof panels
- Risk: less brand personality unless supported by strong photography or logo

## 8. Reseller Notebook

Personal, scrappy, and organized. It feels like the user’s sourcing notebook became an app.

- Palette: notebook paper, black ink, denim blue, highlighter yellow, profit green
- Texture: ruled paper, check marks, margin notes, sticker labels
- Best for: buy list, onboarding, educational content, beta-friendly tone
- Asset direction: notebook + phone compositions, handwritten-style supporting graphics, checklist empty states
- Risk: handwritten elements can reduce perceived professionalism if overused

## 9. Scan Lab

Clean, precise, and data-backed. This presents ShelfMargin as a serious testing tool for sourcing decisions.

- Palette: cool gray, white, black, blue, lime profit accent
- Texture: lab labels, grid overlays, calibrated scan frames, compact metrics
- Best for: dashboard, admin readiness, data accuracy, Amazon integration messaging
- Asset direction: UI closeups, data confidence badges, scan frame overlays, comparison panels
- Risk: could feel detached from the real thrift-store environment

## 10. Shelf Tag System

The whole product identity comes from shelf tags and price stickers. Visual attention goes to small labels, clear prices, and action states.

- Palette: white, black, shelf yellow, sticker red, price-tag blue, muted wood tones
- Texture: adhesive labels, shelf strips, barcode stickers, simple outlines
- Best for: logo, hero, feature icons, app icon, marketing sections
- Asset direction: shelf-label logo mark, price-tag UI chips, book-spine hero scenes, barcode sticker product screenshots
- Risk: yellow can dominate quickly; needs a neutral base and restrained accent use

## Recommended Shortlist

Start with these three:

1. Modern Book Scout: strongest balance of professional, book-native, and approachable.
2. Profit Signal: best for the actual app experience and attention retention.
3. Shelf Tag System: best raw material for logo, icons, and repeatable visual assets.

## Next Step

Create 3 quick concept boards from the shortlist. Each board should include:

- logo/app-mark prompt
- color palette
- homepage hero prompt
- mobile scanner screen prompt
- empty-state prompt
- app-store screenshot prompt
